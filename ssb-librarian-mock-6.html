  3. The Question Palette on the right shows the status of each question using these symbols:
1You have not visited the question yet.
2You have not answered the question.
3You have answered the question.
4Not answered, but marked for review.
5Answered and marked for review. This answer will be evaluated.
  1. To answer, select one option and click Save & Next. If you move to another question without clicking Save & Next, your selection is not saved.
  2. Mark for Review & Next saves your selection (if any) and flags the question so you can come back to it.
  3. Clear Response removes the saved answer of the current question.
  4. Click any number in the palette to jump to that question. On a phone, open the palette with the ☰ Palette button.
  5. Use A− / A+ next to the question number to make the text smaller or larger.
  6. Alerts appear when 30, 10 and 5 minutes are left.
  7. Your progress is kept in this browser, so a page refresh will not lose your answers.
